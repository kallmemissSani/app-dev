# My Favorite Movie: Corpse Bride

## About the Movie

**Corpse Bride** is a dark fantasy animated movie directed by **Tim Burton** and released in 2005. It follows the story of Victor Van Dort, who accidentally marries a deceased bride named Emily while practicing his wedding vows in the forest.

## Why I Like It

I love this movie because of its unique gothic art style, beautiful animation, emotional storyline, and memorable characters. It combines romance, dark humor, and fantasy in a very creative way.

## Favorite Characters

- **Emily** - The mysterious and loving Corpse Bride.

## My Favorite Things About the Movie

1. Its beautiful gothic animation.
2. The combination of dark humor and romance.
3. Emily's emotional story.
4. The memorable soundtrack and music.
